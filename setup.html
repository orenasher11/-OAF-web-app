Cloud setup

About 10 minutes. It's free, and you can do it all from your phone.

1Create your free cloud project

  1. Go to supabase.com and tap Start your project.
  2. Sign up with your email or GitHub.
  3. Tap New project. Name: oaf. Pick a strong database password and save it in your notes.
  4. Region: West US (North California). Plan: Free. Tap Create new project and wait about 2 minutes.
Tip: the Supabase site works best in your phone's browser with Request Desktop Website turned on (tap aA in Safari's address bar).

2Run the setup code

Type your email below. This is the email you'll sign in to the app with. Then copy the code.

  1. In Supabase, open SQL Editor in the left menu, then New query.
  2. Paste the code (touch and hold, then Paste), then tap Run.
  3. It should say Success. If you see a red error, send me a screenshot.

3Make sign-in codes work

In Supabase, open Authentication, then Emails (or Email Templates). Edit both the Confirm signup and the Magic Link templates. Replace everything in the message body with:

<h2>Your sign-in code</h2>
<p>Type this code in the app: <b>{{ .Token }}</b></p>

Tap Save on each template.

Supabase's built-in email sender only allows a few emails per hour. That's fine for you and your partners. If codes stop arriving, tell me and I'll connect a free email service.

4Send me two values

In Supabase, open Project Settings → API (or tap the Connect button at the top). Copy these two and send them to me in our chat:

  • Project URL (looks like https://abcd.supabase.co)
  • anon public key (also called publishable key, a long text starting with eyJ or sb_publishable)
Never send the service_role key, a secret key, or your database password. I only need the two public values above.

Do this once, before you invite your first rep. It makes the cloud itself hide everything a rep should not see: your 1%, your team list, other reps' jobs and who else has an account. Partners you invite as Partner still see everything.

5Invite your partners and reps

In the app, go to More → ☁️ Cloud. Under Invite someone by email, type their email and choose Sales rep: only their own jobs (then pick which rep) or Partner: everything. They open the app, tap Cloud, and sign in with that email.
